# Competitor services: commercial football prediction and value-bet products (insy.io, predictstats.com, and reference tools)

*Research date: 2026-09-28. Pages were read as a normal visitor, with non-essential cookies declined, no account, and no personal or payment data entered. Labels used: **[MARKETING]** = the vendor's own claim, not independently verified; **[VERIFIED-ISH]** = third-party or academic data, or a vendor ledger that can be inspected; **[OBSERVED]** = something I saw on the page myself (for example the tip-ledger data the site sends to every visitor).*

## 1. insy.io: what it is, who runs it, what the "Bukmacherka" listings sell, track record, red flags

### Takeaway
insy.io is not a prediction service. It is a Polish marketplace where creators sell paid Discord/Telegram communities, and it takes a commission. Its "Bukmacherka" category held only three small listings when I checked: two arbitrage scanners and one tipster group, "Betlab - ValuePL". Each one advertises profit claims that nobody has verified ("+19,80% ROI", "without risk"). None has a timestamped public ledger, and they have 2 to 6 members each.

- I found no timestamped, pre-kickoff ledger for BetLab ValuePL. I also could not tell whether the "+19.80% ROI" is before or after the 12% tax.

## 2. predictstats.com: outputs, methodology, cadence, leagues/markets, pricing, track record, affiliation, operator

### Takeaway
PredictStats (PREDICT STATS sp. z o.o., Chełmiec) sells four different things:
- **Value Bets:** a naive "value" screen based on how often an outcome happened in each team's last 25 matches.
- **Moneyflow:** a properly designed scanner that compares Polish bookmakers against a sharp bookmaker. Its guide is methodologically the best Polish-language material I found (de-vigging, sharp limit, CLV archive, an explicit 13.6% break-even under the 12% tax).
- **Insider:** a Telegram tipster group. Its ledger shows a 20.6% yield **before tax** by default, but only about 6% after tax. Most history rows have no pre-kickoff publication timestamp, and some were entered a day after the match. Recent picks are obscure lower-league, women's and basketball games sent 2–29 minutes before kickoff, sold with "insider info".
- **Affiliate:** a Betfan affiliate deal.

**Value Bets module: a statistical frequency model**
- Method: for each match of the day and each market (goals, BTTS, corners, cards, 1X2, double chance; full time and halves), it counts how often the event happened in both teams' recent matches. The default is 25 matches per team, split home/away, or H2H mode. That frequency is compared with the **best bookmaker's** odds, and value is how far the frequency exceeds the implied probability — [predictstats.com/pl/value-bets](https://predictstats.com/pl/value-bets).
- Output per pick: the value deviation (%), average odds, implied %, "our model" %, home/away %, sample size, odds history (Premium). Free users get 3 tips/day; Premium gets 64. A signal disappears when the odds fall below the threshold. The site advises checking in the morning — [predictstats.com/pl/value-bets](https://predictstats.com/pl/value-bets).
- Example on the day I checked [OBSERVED]: Al Arabi vs Al Thaid (UAE Presidents Cup), BTTS at odds 1.73, implied 57.8% vs model 76.0%, shown as "+31.5%". The page advertised "+61 more tips with deviation up to +31%" — [predictstats.com/pl/value-bets](https://predictstats.com/pl/value-bets).
- The site's own caveats: default odds cap of 2.50; +5–15% is where 25-match statistics are reliable; treat >25% with care (small sample, missing key player, odds about to drop); stake flat 1–2%; no AKO accumulators; judge after dozens of bets — [predictstats.com/pl/value-bets](https://predictstats.com/pl/value-bets).

**Moneyflow module: Polish bookmakers vs a sharp bookmaker**
- Moneyflow compares Polish bookmakers' odds in real time with a sharp bookmaker's price. It is framed as catching the minutes-to-hours lag before Polish books follow sharp line moves — [PredictStats Moneyflow guide](https://predictstats.com/pl/poradniki/moneyflow) (updated 9 Sep 2026). The guide never names the sharp bookmaker. Pinnacle and Betfair do not appear anywhere in it [OBSERVED].
- Formulas: fair price = the sharp price with the margin removed proportionally. Edge = soft price / fair price − 1. Edge after tax = soft price × 0.88 / fair − 1 — [Moneyflow guide](https://predictstats.com/pl/poradniki/moneyflow).
- **Tax break-even stated explicitly:** a single bet has EV = 0 when price × 0.88 = fair price, so the edge must be ≥13.6% ("in practice from 14%"). Their table of EV per 100 zł with tax: +3% edge → −9.36 zł; +10% → −3.20 zł; +20% → +5.60 zł. A 5% post-tax edge needs a 19.3% pre-tax edge, and such signals are "rare and short-lived" — [Moneyflow guide](https://predictstats.com/pl/poradniki/moneyflow).
- Three recommended ways around the tax — [Moneyflow guide](https://predictstats.com/pl/poradniki/moneyflow):
  - tax-free promotions, where 2–3% edges become profitable;
  - AKO accumulators with boosts, using 3–5% per leg (example: 3 × +5% plus a 10% boost ≈ +12.1% EV after tax);
  - singles with ≥14% edge.
  The guide warns about variance, boost terms and correlated legs.
- Signal-card fields: edge badge, strength dots, NEW (under 5 minutes old), "confirmed at 2 bookmakers", low liquidity, high odds, better line, previous-price arrow. The table view adds Fair, Sharp and **sharp Limit (€)**. Limit tiers: ≥2,000 € liquid; 500–2,000 € second-tier leagues and side markets; <500 € "paper edge". A "Manipulacje" sort shows lines with limits ≤50 € — [Moneyflow guide](https://predictstats.com/pl/poradniki/moneyflow).
- An **Archive view has a CLV column** (did the signal price beat the close) — [Moneyflow guide](https://predictstats.com/pl/poradniki/moneyflow).
- Filters: bookmakers the user has accounts at, sport (football, basketball, tennis), market, time to kickoff (≤3 h is "mature" sharp pricing), minimum edge, odds range (1.50–3.50 suggested), limit range (≥500 € suggested), best pick per match (dedupe), value-after-tax toggle, sound alert, Telegram alerts for custom filters — [Moneyflow guide](https://predictstats.com/pl/poradniki/moneyflow).
- Staking advice: flat 1–2% on singles and 0.5–1% on AKO, or ¼ Kelly using the fair price. With tax, Kelly on most signals comes out ≤0, which the guide treats as a "don't bet" signal — [Moneyflow guide](https://predictstats.com/pl/poradniki/moneyflow).
- The guide notes that goals and handicap markets produce the most signals because Polish books update them more slowly than 1X2 — [Moneyflow guide](https://predictstats.com/pl/poradniki/moneyflow).
- **Free "Perełki Moneyflow" list:** at most 3 picks per day. They are the *first* three signals of the day where a Polish book prices an event **≥20% above the sharp fair price**, at odds 1.20–2.00. The odds and minute are logged when the pick is found; nothing is recalculated or deleted; settlement uses the logged odds — [predictstats.com/pl/typy-na-dzis/moneyflow](https://predictstats.com/pl/typy-na-dzis/moneyflow).
  - 90-day record shown: 26 won, 18 lost, 6 pushes, 1 pending. Hit rate 59.1%, yield +11.7%, +5.15 units at 1-unit stakes [VERIFIED-ISH: a vendor ledger with timestamps, but I could not audit it].
  - Example picks: Ehime FC +1.75 at **lvbet** 2.00 vs sharp fair 1.62 (+23.7%, sharp limit 300 €, J-League Cup). Altavilla at **totalbet** 1.63 vs fair 1.34 (+21.6%, limit 125 €, Serie D).

### Inferences
- **The headline yield is misleading for Polish users.** It is shown pre-tax by default, while the product is sold for Polish licensed bookmakers. 1.2058 × 0.88 − 1 ≈ +6.1% after tax. That matches the toggle: +11.1k zł ≈ +111u on ≈1,816u staked (373.65 / 0.2058). After tax, 4 of the last 13 months were at or below about 0% (Jul −6.6%, Oct −10.0%, Jun +0.1%, Aug +1.4%, Feb +1.4%).
- **The historical ledger is not verifiable as pre-kickoff.** Before 27.09.2026 there are no publication timestamps, and some rows were created after the matches. That is consistent with results being logged after the fact. It may just be a migration from Telegram, but an outsider cannot rule out selective logging. The Telegram channel history would be the only check.
- **Picks sent minutes before kickoff in thin markets** (women's cups, Polish 4th tier, Israeli Liga Bet), together with "insider info" claims, carry three problems:
  - They cannot be followed at scale: prices move and limits are tiny.
  - They are an integrity/regulatory risk.
  - They are the opposite of Model Buk's stated scope (top-20 European leagues).
- The **Value Bets module is statistically weak.** A 25-match frequency has a standard error of about ±7–10 percentage points. Taking the maximum "deviation" across dozens of markets and hundreds of matches, against the *best* bookmaker's odds, systematically produces spurious +20–30% "value" (winner's curse/selection bias). The site itself warns that >25% values usually mean a small sample.
- **Moneyflow is the part worth copying**, because it is market-based (a sharp de-vigged fair price) and puts tax, liquidity, CLV and freshness in the UI. Its free list's published hit rate and yield (50 settled picks) is statistically meaningless: with odds of about 1.8 the standard error of the yield is roughly ±12–13 pp. Yield 11.7% = 5.15 / 44, which suggests pushes are left out of the denominator. The page does not say whether the figure is before or after tax.
- The company runs Moneyflow (which honestly says singles need ≥14% edge after tax) and at the same time markets Insider's pre-tax 20.6%. That is a conflict of messaging.

## 3. Established sharp-vs-soft value-bet tools (RebelBetting, Trademate, OddsJam, BetBurger, Oddstorm, ArbiScan): how they work and what is verified

### Takeaway
All serious value-bet tools use the same method as Moneyflow: take a sharp price (usually Pinnacle, or a consensus), remove the margin, and flag soft-bookmaker prices above it. Vendors claim yields of about 3–4% per bet, with volume (tens of bets per day) doing the work. The only published per-user results come from affiliate reviewers, and they are mixed. BetBurger's *default* value reference is Bet365/William Hill, not a sharp bookmaker.

### Cited Findings
- **RebelBetting:**
  - Method: compares soft bookmakers with "sharp" ones whose odds adjust to expert money. Pinnacle is named as the sharp that welcomes arbitrage and does not limit winners [MARKETING] — [rebelbetting.com/valuebetting](https://www.rebelbetting.com/valuebetting).
  - Claims: "proven yield" 3.7% per bet; "30% average ROI per month"; 120+ bookmakers scanned 24/7; members placed 21.1 million bets for £17.3 million profit [MARKETING, not audited]. Prices: Starter €99/month (€69 on annual billing), Pro €199/month (€139 on annual billing); 14-day free trial — [rebelbetting.com/valuebetting](https://www.rebelbetting.com/valuebetting).

### Inferences
- The industry-standard claim is about 3–4% pre-tax yield per bet at high volume (30–40 bets/day). Under Polish tax that is roughly 0.88 × 1.037 − 1 ≈ **−8.7%**. **The RebelBetting/Trademate model does not work at Polish licensed bookmakers** unless the edge is ≥13.6% or tax-free promotions or boosts apply. PredictStats' own guide says the same (section 2).
- Tools that use soft books as the reference (BetBurger's default) measure "value" against another soft price. That is weaker evidence than a de-vigged sharp price.

### Gaps

- I found no independent audit of RebelBetting's aggregate member statistics, nor RebelBetting's current bookmaker list for Poland (the list page did not render).

## 5. Typical edge %, bets/day, expected yield, and account limits: vendors vs independent evidence

### Takeaway
Independent studies find **long-run yields of about 3–4% pre-tax** from betting soft bookmakers against a sharp or consensus price. Real-money execution briefly produced 8.5% before accounts were limited. Studies, reviewers and users all agree that **winning accounts get limited within weeks to months**. Vendors quote similar per-bet yields but add volume assumptions (30–40 bets/day) that limits make unrealistic.

### Cited Findings
- **Kaunitz, Zhong & Kreiner (2017), "Beating the bookies with their own numbers":** bet whenever the maximum odds exceed the consensus-implied fair odds by a margin — [arXiv 1710.02824](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.02824) (full PDF text read):
  - 10-year simulation on closing odds (479,440 games, 818 leagues, 2005–2015): **3.5% return**, 44.4% hit rate. A random-bet control returned −3.32%.
  - Simulation on minute-by-minute odds: 9.9% return, mean odds 2.32.
  - Paper trading: 5.5% return, $1,128.50.
  - **Real money: 265 bets of $50, +$957.50, 8.5% return.** Paper plus real combined: 672 bets, +$2,086, 6.2%.
  - The consensus probability was an extremely accurate proxy for true probability (regression slopes about 1.00–1.08).
- Kaunitz et al. on limits: a few months after real-money betting started, bookmakers "severely limited" their accounts. Stakes were cut below $50 and wagers were sent to "manual inspection", so the experiment ended. The authors note that limited games introduce non-random sampling bias — [arXiv 1710.02824](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.02824). A press quote says limits started "a few weeks" after real-money trading — [SportsHandle](https://sportshandle.com/sportsbooks-vs-academics-one-wins-battle/).
- The authors' repository says deploying the strategy was not worth the effort given the time and the monetary reward — [GitHub BeatTheBookie](https://github.com/Lisandro79/BeatTheBookie).
- **Buchdahl "Wisdom of the Crowd"** (soft odds vs Pinnacle's margin-free odds), as reported by the Pinnacle Odds Dropper (a commercial tool) — [pinnacleoddsdropper.com](https://www.pinnacleoddsdropper.com/blog/joseph-buchdahl's-betting-strategy) [VERIFIED-ISH, secondary]:
  - Retrospective backtest, about 67,000 odds: expected 2.2%, actual 3.3%.
  - Live results from August 2015, about 18,000 value odds, rule "bet only if EV > 102%": expected **4.0%**, actual **3.7%**.
  - Buchdahl acknowledges that accounts get restricted and suggests disguising betting.
- Football-Data stopped posting weekly Wisdom-of-Crowd selections from 2025/26 **because Pinnacle discontinued its free API odds feed** — [football-data.co.uk](https://www.football-data.co.uk/wisdom_of_crowd_bets.php).
- Buchdahl's tests of margin-removal methods on Pinnacle closing odds: equal-margin removal returned −0.56% (it misses favourite–longshot bias), "margin proportional to odds" about +0.1%. Choosing a de-vig method that accounts for the favourite–longshot bias matters — [Wisdom of the Crowd PDF](https://www.football-data.co.uk/The_Wisdom_of_the_Crowd_updated.pdf).
- Vendor volume claims: RebelBetting assumes 40 bets/day and >3% yield [MARKETING] — [RebelBetting calculator](https://www.rebelbetting.com/faq/value-betting-profit-calculator). BetLab ValuePL advertises 5–8 signals/week [MARKETING] — [insy.io/communities/betlab](https://insy.io/communities/betlab). PredictStats' free Moneyflow list caps at 3 per day and often has 0 qualifying (≥20% edge) lines at a given scan — [predictstats.com/pl/typy-na-dzis/moneyflow](https://predictstats.com/pl/typy-na-dzis/moneyflow).
- Limits in practice:
  - An affiliate tester was limited at Bet365/SkyBet but could still win £50–100 per bet in English lower leagues — [smartsportstrader.com](https://smartsportstrader.com/trademate-sports-review/).
  - OddsJam users report limits after days — [Trustpilot OddsJam](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/oddsjam.com) (search summary).
  - RebelBetting's own pitch is only that value betting stays "under the radar longer" than arbitrage — [rebelbetting.com](https://www.rebelbetting.com/valuebetting).
- **Polish context:** Polish betting guides say bookmakers use stake limits against players using value betting or similar strategies. They also say STS reportedly does not close accounts just for winning, but cuts winners' stakes to "dozens or even tens of złoty" — [bet1x2.pl](https://bet1x2.pl/czy-sts-usuwa-konta-graczom-ktorzy-czesto-wygrywaja/), [surebety.pl](https://surebety.pl/artykul/limity-stawek/) (search summaries; affiliate-style sites).
- PredictStats separates the **sharp's market limit** (a liquidity signal) from the **user's account limit** at a Polish book, and does not claim to solve the latter — [Moneyflow guide](https://predictstats.com/pl/poradniki/moneyflow).

### Inferences
- Realistic numbers for Model Buk. At Polish books with 12% tax:
  - Only signals with ≥13.6% edge against a de-vigged sharp price are +EV as singles.
  - Such edges are rare. They cluster in thin markets and lower leagues, where the sharp price is less reliable (limit <500 €) and stale-price voids and account limits are likely.
  - In top-20 European leagues on main markets, sharp-vs-PL-book gaps will usually be well below 14%.
  - So "a few high-confidence bets per day with meaningful profit" is unlikely from pure soft-vs-sharp price-lag betting on singles. The realistic routes are:
    - tax-free promotions;
    - boosted AKO accumulators built from +3–5% legs, where correlation and terms need checking;
    - a genuinely better model than the market, which the evidence says is rare.
- Model Buk should budget for **limits within weeks to months** at any Polish book it beats consistently. Kaunitz and Buchdahl both confirm limits even at modest $50 stakes.
- With Pinnacle's free API gone, a sharp reference price for Model Buk needs a paid odds feed or exchange data.

### Gaps
- I found no source that quantifies how quickly specific Polish licensed bookmakers (STS, Fortuna, Betclic, Superbet, LV BET, Betfan, Fuksiarz) limit value bettors.
- No source covered whether Polish books void "obvious error" prices of +20% against the market.
- Separately from the 12% stake tax, winnings above a threshold may carry an additional tax in Poland. I did not research this here.

## 7. Independent verification infrastructure (Blogabet, Tipstrr, Pyckio, Betting Expert) and what verified records show

### Takeaway
Third-party tipster platforms verify by taking picks *before* kickoff at recorded (often Pinnacle) odds and settling them automatically. That is the standard neither insy.io's nor PredictStats' tipster groups meet. Records highlighted on these platforms suffer from survivorship bias. The showcased long-run winners have about 11–13% yield over roughly 750–1,100 picks, often in niche sports or lower leagues, and they are the survivors of a large pool.

### Cited Findings
- Blogabet and Pyckio provide third-party verification: tipsters submit picks before the event, and the platform records results and computes statistics — [search summary incl. blogabet.com guide](https://blogabet.com/betting-guide/blogabet-tutorials/betting-blogs-and-tipster-stats-explained). Blogabet announced changes to its verification — [blogabet.com announcement 207](https://blogabet.com/announcement/207/blogabet-verification-is-changing) (the page returned 403; content not read).
- Pyckio uses Pinnacle odds; Joseph Buchdahl advises Pyckio. Pyckio's rating algorithm (0–5) penalises luck to estimate whether profit reflects skill — [Pyckio blog (search listing)](https://blog.pyckio.com/en/eg-pinnacle-closing-odds/), [Pyckio rating help](https://help.pyckio.com/en/pyckio-rating/).
- Showcased Pyckio records [VERIFIED-ISH, but selected winners] — [Smart Betting Club](https://smartbettingclub.com/blog/our-latest-tipster-review-looks-at-a-service-with-a-12-7-roi-at-pinnacle/) (search summary):
  - tennis tipster: 1,092 bets, +139 points, 12.7% ROI at Pinnacle;
  - football (English National League) tipster: 11.2% yield over 756 picks;
  - volleyball tipster: 12.2% over 1,053 picks.
- Pinnacle's article on the tipster marketplace and survivorship bias: tipsters who survive past a given point look far more likely to be profitable (reported as >80% after day 200), which is a survivorship artefact — [Pinnacle](https://pinnacle.com/en/betting-articles/educational/tipster-marketplace-and-survivorship-bias/hgz26fmjeq29gn9g) (page unreachable; search summary only).
- PodziemneTypy (the Polish tipster aggregator linked to BetLab and PredictStats) claims "verified statistics" from "a special system" but documents no method or odds source — [podziemnetypy.com](https://podziemnetypy.com/en).

### Inferences
- Model Buk should apply this standard to itself:
  - an append-only ledger with the pick timestamp before kickoff;
  - the odds and bookmaker actually available at that time;
  - the stake rule;
  - settlement;
  - the closing sharp price, and so CLV;
  - reports both before and after tax.
- Showcased tipster records (roughly 11–13% yields) are an upper tail and should not set Model Buk's expectations.

### Gaps
- I could not read Blogabet's current verification rules, Tipstrr or Betting Expert pages, or the original Pinnacle survivorship study (they were blocked or unreachable).
- No platform-wide statistic was found for the share of football tipsters beating Pinnacle closing lines over 1,000+ picks.

### Inferences
**Copy (product/UX)**
1. **Show after-tax EV as the default number:** edge_after_tax = odds × 0.88 / fair − 1, with the 13.6% break-even for singles shown next to it. Never show pre-tax yield as the headline. (Pattern from the [Moneyflow guide](https://predictstats.com/pl/poradniki/moneyflow); anti-pattern in [Insider](https://predictstats.com/pl/vip-tips).)
2. **Signal card fields:**
   - edge, fair price, sharp price and sharp liquidity/limit;
   - time to kickoff;
   - price-movement arrow, freshness (NEW under 5 minutes), "confirmed at ≥2 books";
   - best pick per match (dedupe);
   - signal expiry when the price drops below the threshold.
   ([Moneyflow guide](https://predictstats.com/pl/poradniki/moneyflow))
3. **Filter by the user's own bookmaker accounts,** with push/Telegram alerts, because edges disappear in minutes. ([Moneyflow guide](https://predictstats.com/pl/poradniki/moneyflow))
4. **Keep an immutable public ledger:** log the odds and the minute at signal time, never recalculate or delete, settle at the logged odds, add a CLV column, and report pushes and tax explicitly. (The "Perełki" pattern at [predictstats.com/pl/typy-na-dzis/moneyflow](https://predictstats.com/pl/typy-na-dzis/moneyflow), plus Pyckio-style pre-event verification.)
5. **Staking:** ¼ Kelly on the fair price or 1–2% flat. Show Kelly ≤ 0 as "don't bet this single under tax". Add a bankroll/variance simulator that uses *after-tax* inputs. ([Moneyflow guide](https://predictstats.com/pl/poradniki/moneyflow))
6. **Tax-aware alternatives, clearly labelled:** tax-free promotions and boosted AKOs, with correlation checks (one leg per match) and boost-terms warnings. ([Moneyflow guide](https://predictstats.com/pl/poradniki/moneyflow))

**Copy (methodology)**
7. **Use a de-vigged sharp or consensus price as "truth" and judge Model Buk by CLV against it** (Kaunitz: consensus is an extremely accurate probability proxy — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.02824)). Use a de-vig method that handles favourite–longshot bias, not equal-margin removal ([Buchdahl PDF](https://www.football-data.co.uk/The_Wisdom_of_the_Crowd_updated.pdf)).
8. **Weight signals by sharp liquidity:** treat edges on lines with sharp limits under 500 € as low confidence ([Moneyflow guide](https://predictstats.com/pl/poradniki/moneyflow)). The very large "edges" (+20% at limits of 125–300 €) come from thin markets.
9. **Plan for a replacement sharp-odds source.** Pinnacle's free API is gone ([football-data.co.uk](https://www.football-data.co.uk/wisdom_of_crowd_bets.php)).

**Avoid**
10. Pre-tax headline yields, hit-rate marketing ("85% accuracy"), "guaranteed", "without risk", "up to 100%" ([betlab.pl](https://betlab.pl/pl), [insy BezRyzyka](https://insy.io/communities/bukmacherka)).
11. Screenshot or Telegram "proof", or ledgers without pre-kickoff timestamps and with rows created after matches ([insy Betlab FAQ](https://insy.io/communities/betlab), [PredictStats Insider data](https://predictstats.com/pl/vip-tips)).
12. **Naive frequency "value"** (last-N-matches % vs best odds). Taking the maximum over many markets manufactures spurious edges ([value-bets](https://predictstats.com/pl/value-bets)). If Model Buk uses its own model probabilities, it should shrink them toward the market price and validate them out of sample by CLV.
13. **Last-minute picks in obscure leagues sold as "insider info"** ([vip-tips](https://predictstats.com/pl/vip-tips), [trafiamy.com](https://trafiamy.com/)). They cannot be verified, have tiny limits, carry integrity risk, and are outside Model Buk's top-20-league scope.
14. **Undisclosed affiliate conflicts:** PredictStats has a Betfan affiliate deal ([partners](https://predictstats.com/pl/partners)); PredictZ uses free-bet offers ([predictz](https://www.predictz.com/)). If Model Buk ever links to bookmakers, it should disclose this and never rank books by commission.
15. **Unrealistic volume assumptions** (40 bets/day; "double in 3 months" — [RebelBetting calculator](https://www.rebelbetting.com/faq/value-betting-profit-calculator)). Limits arrive within weeks to months ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.02824)).

**Expectation-setting for Model Buk**
- Independent evidence points to about 3–4% pre-tax yield for soft-vs-sharp value ([POD/Buchdahl](https://www.pinnacleoddsdropper.com/blog/joseph-buchdahl's-betting-strategy), [Kaunitz](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.02824)). That is −EV after 12% tax.
- The one Polish tipster ledger I could inspect shows about 6% after tax, pre-tax 20.6% ([Insider](https://predictstats.com/pl/vip-tips)). It is unverifiable before 27.09.2026 and comes from niche leagues.
- So "a few high-confidence value bets with meaningful profit" in the top-20 leagues at Polish books should be treated as a hypothesis to test with a timestamped CLV ledger, not as a product promise.

### Gaps
- I did not test any paid product, so data quality, latency and signal volume of Moneyflow, ArbiScan or RebelBetting at Polish books are unknown.
- No independent Polish-language review or forum thread assessing insy.io communities, BetLab or PredictStats was found.
